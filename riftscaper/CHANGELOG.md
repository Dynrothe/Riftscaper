# Changelog

What's changed in Riftscaper, newest first, for players: the title screen shows it too (What's new,
in its corner). One short bullet a change, under Added, Changed or Fixed.

## Unreleased

## 0.3.1 - 2026-11-23

### Added

- Export saves the chosen hero to a file, and Import brings heroes in from files, to keep them safe or play them in another browser.

## 0.3.0 - 2026-11-18

### Added

- A potion belt of four places, drunk with 1 to 4: a life potion first, a mana potion second, any in the last two.
- The Apothecary stitches the belt's third and fourth places on, for the Wine of Zamorak K'ril drops and the Jar of darkness Skotizo drops.
- Potions as loot: five grades of life and mana potion, a rejuvenation potion and six combat potions.
- Magic potions, with affixes that change how they recover and what they do while they work.
- Eight unique potions, three of them found only in the Heart of the Rift.
- Herbs fall in the rifts and go in your pouch with your runes.
- The Apothecary, on the square's west side: brews potions up a grade, instils them to drink themselves, and makes elixirs.
- Elixirs: one at a time, for ten minutes in the rifts, with a stat and more experience.
- The herblore way past the tree's rim, between Dexterity and Intelligence, ending in the Barbarian Herblore keystone.
- The ladder: past rift 16 each rift is rolled from its parts, a land, a host and a guardian of the host's.
- Fifteen hosts hold the ladder's rifts, from Bandos's warband to the Abyss, with forty-one new monsters and eight new guardians.
- Ladder lands mix two biomes now and then, and one in five lies at dusk.
- The Archmage's offers past rift 16 show each rift's land, host and guardian.
- Twelve new monster mods on the ladder: Infernal, Frostbound, Charged, Blinking, Volleying, Raising, Commanding, Binding, Grasping, Molten, Draining and Shielding.
- Ailments from monster mods: burning, chilled, shocked and rooted, shown beside your buffs.
- Four potion suffixes that end an ailment and keep it off: of Dousing, of Heat, of Grounding and of Footing.
- Ladder monsters with more mods drop more and better loot.
- An encounter in every ladder rift, shown in the Archmage's offers: a fleeing impling, a Wilderness hunter, the Fight Caves or the Barrows.
- Implings flee and are gone after 15 seconds; catch one and its jar spills open.
- Wilderness hunters find you from far off and never give up; what they wear is yours.
- The Fight Caves: waves of TzHaar for tokkul, doubling with each wave you dare.
- The Barrows: open three brothers' sarcophagi and slay them to open their chest.
- Tokkul, kept in the pouch.
- Superiors: any ladder kill has a 1 in 200 chance of calling up a much tougher foe.
- Wilderness bosses roam one ladder rift in four, hunting you.
- Ladder guardians have a mod, two from rift 30; three are theirs alone: Enraging, Summoning and Warded.
- Ladder guardians have a new special each: a charge, a summon, or a field of fire or frost.
- Sets: the six Barrows brothers' and obsidian, each with bonuses for two, three and four pieces worn.
- The Barrows chest drops a slain brother's set pieces, and each brother now and then his own.
- TzHaar-Hur-Tel, at the square's north-west stall, sells obsidian for tokkul.
- Chances to cast a skill when you strike, are struck or kill, on uniques, sets and runewords.
- Affixes that add levels to your skill gems: all of them, or one kind's.
- A rift-touched seventh tier of affixes, rarely, on items of level 65 and up.
- Elite bases on the ladder: godswords, Torva, Ancestral, Masori, Tumeken's shadow and more.
- Nine high runes from the ladder, water to sunfire, for runewords only.
- Runewords: Aubury inscribes fourteen on a normal item with exactly the right sockets.
- The transmuting circle at Aubury's: three runes, herbs or riftstones into the next, rejuvenation potions, and elite bases.
- Levels go on to 99: each past 60 is a passive point and nothing else, about a ladder rift's worth.
- Pest Control on the ladder: break four portals round a Void Knight before the pests overrun him, for Void seals.
- God Wars skirmishes on the ladder: two gods' armies at war, and every follower you slay is their god's kill count.
- Abyssal breaches on the ladder: touch one and the Abyss pours out inside its ring for 22 seconds, dropping abyssal ashes.
- Guardians of the Rift on the ladder: bring the Great Guardian essence from what you slay before time's up, for abyssal pearls.
- The encounter under way shows its state under the rift's name.
- Four new pinnacles: Graardor, Kree'arra, K'ril and Zilyana, each opened with forty of their god's kill count.
- The Abyssal Sire's pinnacle, opened with a hundred abyssal ashes; it drops the Abyssal whip too.
- The generals guard themselves with their own bodyguards, and the Sire with its spawn.
- Fifteen new pinnacle uniques: the four godswords (or), the gods' d'hide bodies and stoles, and the Sire's dagger, bludgeon and lantern.
- The Fortis Colosseum, a pinnacle open to anyone past rift 16: twelve waves, then Sol Heredit.
- Glories in the Colosseum: after each wave take one of three, a harder rule that makes the rest of the run pay better.
- Five Colosseum uniques: Dizana's quiver, Tonalztics of Ralos and the Sunfire fanatic armour.
- Sets: the Void Knight equipment, the Raiments of the Eye and the Graceful outfit.
- The Void Knight at the square's south-east stall sells the Void Knight equipment for Void seals.
- Apprentice Felix at the square's north-east stall sells the Raiments of the Eye for abyssal pearls.
- Graceful pieces drop from the ladder's guardians, and now and then its rares.
- The Prayer tab: thirteen prayers, one protection and one other at once, each keeping a share of your mana while it's on.
- Hirelings: the Mercenary Captain, by the square's north-west lantern, hires a Barbarian, an Archer, a Battle mage or a White Knight to fight beside you.
- Give your hireling a weapon of its kind, a helm and a body; it comes armed, and lives under your prayers.
- A fallen hireling stays down till the Captain raises them for gold.
- Reldo's quests: one for each of the first eight zones, each leaving something for good, from a passive point to more life.
- The gem trader adds a socket for nothing, or imbues a normal item rare, with Reldo's favour.
- Resistances to fire, cold and lightning, on gear and talismans, held to 75%.
- The rift web: past rift 16, the Archmage's rifts at your deepest are places on a web, each with a bounty, and the lane you clear opens the next.
- Something in the meadows lays eggs. Aubury's circle may know what they're for.
- A unique, a set piece or a high rune from Law up lands with a pillar of light in its colour, and a sound of its own.
- Rift-touched uniques: a unique found on the ladder above its own level has its numbers lifted to the level it dropped at.
- Aubury's circle makes OSRS's upgrades of a unique and its part: the Abyssal tentacle, Arclight, Emberlight, Primordial boots and more.
- Parts for the upgrades drop from the ladder's hosts and guardians, and wait in the pouch.
- The circle imbues the Berserker, Archers, Seers and Warrior rings, the Ring of suffering and the Skull sceptre.
- The Max cape: Reldo's at level 99, and the circle sews it into an Infernal cape or a Blessed Dizana's quiver.
- Awakened pinnacles: each pinnacle again, harder, opened with its key and an Awakener's orb.
- Awakened guardians drop two uniques every time, Chromium ingots, and the generals their vestiges.
- The Ultor, Venator, Magus and Bellator rings, made of a vestige and three Chromium ingots.
- The orrery, in the wizards' study: your firsts on the ladder light its spheres, to spend on what the ladder holds.
- The collection log, in the Log tab: a slot for everything there is to find.
- Combat achievements in six tiers, for every guardian, the ladder, the encounters and the raid.
- Ghommal's hilts, opened by achievement points, each a convenience.
- Pets: guardians drop their OSRS pets now and then, and one follows you.
- The Void Knight makes the Void Knight top and robe elite, for seals.
- TzHaar-Hur-Tel sells an Uncut onyx, which the circle cuts into a fury or a Ring of suffering.
- Apprentice Felix sells talismans for pearls, now and then a unique one.
- Fortunato's elite gamble past level 60: elite bases at the deepest area level you've reached.
- Father Lawrence pays an Ancient shard besides for a task past rift 16.
- The Kraken guards Waterbirth's dagannoth, a whirlpool till you come near.
- Zulrah guards Karamja's jungle, turning through its three forms.
- The Tombs of Amascut, a raid: four paths off the Nexus in any order (Akkha, Ba-Ba, Kephri, Zebak), then the Wardens in the Nexus.
- Ten invocations for the Tombs, chosen at the Archmage's, raise its raid level and what it pays.
- Three Tombs uniques: Lightbearer, Elidinis' ward (f) and the Keris partisan of the sun.

### Changed

- Rifts are laid out like Diablo's and Path of Exile's maps: arenas joined by walled lanes, a road to the guardian, chests in the dead ends.
- The two flasks are now the belt's first two potions; your keys for them still work.
- Uniques roll their numbers, each within a fifth, and a Soul rune rolls them again.
- Uniques and set pieces drop unidentified; Reldo, in the city, reads them for nothing.
- A ladder rift cleared gives one passive point, not two: levels past 60 give the rest, and you keep the points you had.
- Shops and the gamble stay at level 60 for heroes past it; the folk's tasks past rift 16 pay at their depth.
- The Graceful boots unique is now the Graceful outfit's boots too, its numbers fixed.
- Each Torment lap takes 15% off your resistances, to 60% from rift 33.
- Fire, cold and lightning from the ladder's monster mods and guardians' fields go through armour; resistances stop them.
- An Elemental rift's extra damage is fire, cold and lightning, a third each, and resisted.
- Runes, herbs and spoils are in the pack's new Pouch tab, so the pack is shorter.
- The rift's box and the tasks' are smaller: a line for each thing, the modifiers on one, and the tasks fold away.
- The collection log is smaller, opens in the middle of the screen and can be moved by its title; so can the orrery.
- Runefall is rebuilt round the tear, its crater in a dais of flagstones: you come home beside it, the Archmage, Reldo and the bank, with the shops round the one square.
- The Mercenary Captain is at the barracks by the south gate; TzHaar-Hur-Tel, the Void Knight and Felix are in the strangers' camp.
- Every door in the city has a way to its street or lane, and every keeper and stall faces its customers.
- Aubury keeps his own rune shop from Varrock, its door open on the tower street and his stock piled behind it.

### Fixed

- The collection log no longer opens in the corner under the rift's box.
- The open side tab is the client's red stone, not a stone with one corner rounded.
- Shrines stand on flat ground and face you, rather than hanging off slopes and cliffs with their backs showing.

## 0.2.0 - 2026-11-14

### Added

- Tasks from the city's folk: take up to three, and whoever set one pays you when it's done.
- Uniques that change how you play: each has a gem's trait, a keystone or a power of its own.
- Chests in every rift, a quarter of them trapped.
- Shrines: the four gods' altars, each a blessing for 40 seconds.
- A guarded hoard in most rifts, which opens once its guards are dead.
- The map marks the chests, shrines and hoards you've seen.
- Riftstones from rift 9: rifts with their modifiers woven in, reworked with runes, opened by the Archmage.
- A second guardian for every land, met on the odd laps past rift 8.
- The Heart of the Rift: bring the Heartstone's four shards, dropped from rift 17, to face the Corporeal Beast.
- Five pinnacle uniques that drop only in the Heart.
- Six wings past the passive tree's rim, one off each class's side, with thirteen new keystones.
- A taste of each wing near its class's start: three nodes off the inner ring.
- Talisman sockets on the tree, and talismans to set in them, dropped in the rifts.
- Six unique talismans that change the passives in their radius.
- Notables whose numbers hang on what you hold, what you're doing or what you hit.
- Aubury enchants rings and amulets with a notable of the tree, for Cosmic runes and gold.

### Changed

- Past depth 16, monsters' life grows about 6% a depth, not 17%.
- Past depth 16, monsters' damage and armour stop growing.
- Two passive points, not one, for each new depth cleared past 16.
- Every map is lit as by day, each with its own light, and no ring of light round your hero.
- Eighteen notables do something new: a forged gem's trait, or more with the right weapon or shield.
- Starting your passive tree over is free, once, as the tree has changed.
- Skeletons raised by Gravebound deal your thrall damage too.

### Fixed

- Barrage linked with Volley keeps its tight spread, so all six arrows hit one enemy.
